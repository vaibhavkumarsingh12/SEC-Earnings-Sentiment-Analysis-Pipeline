Sentiment Methodology

Our pipeline employs dual sentiment engines to yield robust NLP indices:

  • FinBERT (Transformer): Specialized fine-tuned language model based on BERT. Computes precise, context-aware financial sentiment vectors. Polarity is calculated as Positive - Negative.
  • Loughran-McDonald (LM): Dictionary-based lexicon tailored for financial terminology. Net score is calculated as (Positive - Negative) / Total Words in the document.

Market Aligned CAR Metrics

Cumulative Abnormal Returns (CAR) represent the sum of the differences between the banking stock returns and the broader sector benchmark (XLF) return:

The window is centered on the filing date T (from trading day T-1 through T+3) to capture pre-filing information leakage and subsequent post-release adjustments.

EPS Surprise is computed as (Reported EPS - Estimated EPS) / |Estimated EPS| * 100 using aligned quarterly reports.
